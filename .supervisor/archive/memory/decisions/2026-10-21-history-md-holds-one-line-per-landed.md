`history.md` is append-only: one line per landed roadmap item or milestone, never per commit or
dispatch, with date, version or commit, title, goals served and pointers to the archived plan and
its decision entries. Written only by the curator's roadmap step. `roadmap.md` holds what is next,
so there is no roadmap archive.
