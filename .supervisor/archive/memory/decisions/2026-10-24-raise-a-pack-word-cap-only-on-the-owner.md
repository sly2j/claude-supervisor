A cap raise, for `supervisor.md` or a skill, lands on the smallest multiple of 10 that fits the file, and only when the owner says so. Existing rules are not trimmed to make room for a rule the owner asked for. The Codex critic raised the supervisor.md Main cap and the skill cap this way.
Rejected: trimming existing rules to fit; a blanket raise (for example by 100).
