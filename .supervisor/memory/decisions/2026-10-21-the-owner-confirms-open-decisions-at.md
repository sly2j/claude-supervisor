Owner decision, taken before v2.0. Open decisions in a memory stage are not settled by the lead or planner alone: the owner confirms them when the stage is planned. Each stage is then implemented with the minimum rule text, so the pack's agents and skills gain only the rules the stage needs. Consequences: plans for Stages 4-5 list their open decisions for owner confirmation, and edits under pack/ stay short.
