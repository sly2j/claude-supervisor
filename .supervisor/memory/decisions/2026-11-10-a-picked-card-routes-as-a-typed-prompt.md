The supervisor's normal routing decides the lead, chain or quick tier; `plan:` is set only when a plan comes out of it.
Why: cards vary from a one-line fix to a program, and a card should not add a stage a typed request would skip.
Rejected: a forced planner stage on pickup; claiming quick-tier cards (they finish with `close-out task <card>`).
