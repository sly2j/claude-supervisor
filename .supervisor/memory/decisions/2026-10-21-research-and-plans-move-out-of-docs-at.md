D3. At Stage 4 research and plans move from `docs/` to `.supervisor/working/research/` and
`working/plans/` for new programs, plus a one-time migration of existing `docs/` and
`docs/archive/` (the research recommended new programs only). Not yet done: until then plans and
research live in `docs/` and are archived to `docs/archive/` by the curator. Reusable by other
projects as a curator procedure.
